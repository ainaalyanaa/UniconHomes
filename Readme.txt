📌 Project Description

Developed a comprehensive Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system for Unicon Homes, designed to streamline business operations, enhance customer engagement, and improve sales tracking.

The system includes end-to-end CRM functionalities, enabling efficient management of leads, customers, communications, and sales processes within a centralized platform.



🚀 Key Features

* Lead Management
  Capture, track, and convert leads with detailed information and status updates.

* Customer Management
  Maintain complete customer profiles, interaction history, and contact details.

* Sales Pipeline
  Monitor deal progress through different stages with clear visibility.

* Communication Tracking
  Record calls, messages, and follow-ups for better customer engagement.

* Segmentation & Analytics
  Advanced segmentation (e.g., RFM) and insights for targeted marketing.

* Social Media Integration
  Manage and monitor social posts and engagement directly within the CRM.

* User Roles & Permissions
  Role-based access control to ensure secure and organized workflows.

* Dashboard & Reports
  Real-time insights and performance tracking through interactive dashboards.



🛠️ Tech Stack

* Backend: Laravel / PHP
* Frontend: HTML, Tailwind, CSS, JavaScript
* Database: MySQL
* API Integration for modular and scalable architecture


🎯 Objective

To build a scalable and user-friendly CRM solution that helps Unicon Homes efficiently manage customer relationships, automate workflows, and drive business growth.
